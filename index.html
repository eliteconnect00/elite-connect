FAQ

Frequently asked questions

For employers

How quickly can you provide staff?

We give you a realistic timeline as soon as we receive your request – it depends on the position, the number of workers and the desired start date.

Which positions can you fill?

Skilled and general positions in hospitality (HoReCa), construction, logistics and warehousing, transport, manufacturing, agriculture and cleaning. If your sector is not listed, just ask us.

How are candidates selected?

We select candidates according to your requirements – qualification, experience, language skills and documents. We interview them, check their information and prepare them for the interview with you.

Do you organize transport?

Yes. We organize the workers' transport to your location.

Do you support the worker after arrival?

Yes. During the first weeks we stay in contact with the worker and help with initial accommodation, registration with the authorities, health insurance and a bank account, so the start goes smoothly.

For candidates

Do I have to pay anything?

You pay nothing for our placement service – we do not charge candidates any fee. If a job involves costs you cover yourself (for example travel or accommodation, when the employer does not provide them), we tell you clearly and in writing before you accept the offer and before departure.

What documents do I need?

A valid ID card or passport and a CV. Depending on the job, also proof of qualifications, a driving licence or other certificates. We tell you exactly which documents the position requires.

Do I need to speak German or Italian?

It is not mandatory, but it is an advantage: basic English, German or Italian helps, depending on the country. The exact requirements depend on the position and the employer.
